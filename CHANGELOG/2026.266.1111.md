## Summary

Consumer leg of the **engine provider class** cutover (spec#144/#145, sdk#272 — both merged). Two spec cutovers land in this consumer at once, both surfaced by the pin bump.

1. **Engine is now `#EngineName`.** The deploy-dispatch teardown path read `req.Node.Engine` straight into a string; it now converts explicitly (`engine = string(req.Node.Engine)`), matching the sdk's own reads.
